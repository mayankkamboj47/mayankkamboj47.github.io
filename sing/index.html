How it works

  • Sing plays the melody and listens to you. Your voice is the line: green is in tune, yellow is close, red is off.
  • Tracks: load any song from your phone. The app works out the melody so you can sing along and get scored, and prepares a karaoke version by cutting out the singer. The Singer's voice slider brings them back as a quiet guide, and Hear vocals only plays just the singer so you can learn the tune.
  • Range finder (the Range button at the top) moves every melody into a key that fits your voice.
  • Drag the slider under the roll to practise from any point.
  • Wired earphones give the most accurate results. On the phone speaker, turn "I'm using earphones" off so the mic ignores the music.
  • If your voice line looks early or late, adjust Sync offset in Settings.
